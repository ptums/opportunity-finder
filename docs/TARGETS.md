# Target industries

Decided by Peter on 2026-10-05. Sources and scoring should aim here. HN proved too engineering-heavy.

**Rule of thumb:** prefer problems where the software would **not** have to hold sensitive regulated data.

## In scope

| Industry | Typical manual pain | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Nonprofits | Donor tracking, grant reporting, volunteer scheduling, Form 990 prep | Orgs serving children or patients |
| Local government | Permits, procurement, records requests, council agendas | Police and courts (CJIS); long sales cycles |
| Trades and home services (HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, cleaning) | Quoting, scheduling, invoicing, chasing payments | Products that process payments themselves |
| Property management (small landlords) | Maintenance requests, leases, rent tracking | Payment processing; fair-housing rules |
| Associations, clubs, churches, membership groups | Dues, events, member lists, volunteers | — |
| Events and venues | Bookings, vendors, staffing | — |
| Small farms and agriculture | Records, compliance logs, sales channels | — |

## Out of scope

| Area | Regulation to avoid |
|---|---|
| Healthcare, including healthcare admin | HIPAA, Medicaid/Medicare rules |
| Education records | FERPA |
| Children's data, childcare | COPPA, state licensing |
| Payments, lending, payroll | GLBA, PCI, money-transmitter laws |
| Police and justice | CJIS |
| Federal government as a customer | FedRAMP |
